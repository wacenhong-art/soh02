reconstruct the distribution of battery degradation features, thereby expanding the sample space and effectively
characterizing battery aging behavior. Second, a collaborative framework for SOH estimation is introduced based
on Seasonal-Trend decomposition using Loess (STL). In this framework, the trend and seasonal components are
modeled separately using TimesNet, while the residual component is processed with iTransformer. Finally, a
generative-augmented multi-modal temporal collaborative framework is proposed, which deeply integrates
small-sample data augmentation with interpretable temporal decomposition to estimate battery SOH under data-

high estimation accuracy, with root mean square error all within 1 %, substantially outperforming existing data-
driven approaches such as LSTM, CNN, and Transformer.
